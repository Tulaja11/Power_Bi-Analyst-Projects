# Customer Behaviour Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, subscription trends, and shopping preferences across various product categories.

## Dashboard Highlights

- KPIs: Total Customers (3900), Total Purchase Amount, Average Purchase Value, and Average Review Rating
- Charts: Sales by Category, Gender Distribution, Subscription Analysis, Seasonal Purchases, Payment Methods, and Shipping Preferences
- Filters: Category, Gender, Season, Subscription Status, and Location for interactive analysis

## Data Source

| File | Description |
|--------|------------|
| customer_shopping_behavior.csv | Customer demographics, purchase details, subscription status, payment methods, discounts, ratings, and shopping behavior data |

## Key Insights

- Clothing is one of the most frequently purchased product categories.
- Customers with active subscriptions show higher purchasing frequency.
- Credit Card and PayPal are among the most preferred payment methods.
- Discount campaigns positively influence customer purchases.
- Seasonal trends impact customer spending patterns and product demand.
