# Insurance Claims Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze insurance claim patterns, premium trends, claim severity, and customer demographics. The dashboard helps insurance companies identify risk factors, understand claim behavior, and improve decision-making.

## Dashboard Highlights

- KPIs: Total Claims, Total Premium Amount, Total Claim Adjustments, and Total Policies
- Charts: Claims by Severity, Claims by Region, Age-wise Claim Analysis, Premium vs Claims Analysis, and Regional Claim Distribution
- Filters: Policy Type, Region, Marital Status, and Source of Lead for interactive drill-down

## Data Source

| File | Description |
|--------|------------|
| Insurance Claims Dataset | Customer demographics, policy details, premium amounts, claim frequency, claim severity, claim adjustments, and regional insurance data |

## Key Insights

- Certain regions generate a higher number of insurance claims than others.
- High-severity claims contribute significantly to total claim costs.
- Claim frequency varies across different policy types.
- Customer age groups show different claim behavior patterns.
- Premium amount and claim frequency have a measurable relationship.
