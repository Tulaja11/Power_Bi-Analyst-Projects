# Supermart Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze supermarket sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product line profitability, and payment trends. The dashboard helps businesses identify top-performing products, customer preferences, and revenue-generating segments.

## Dashboard Highlights

- KPIs: Total Sales, Total Quantity Sold, Gross Income, and Average Customer Rating
- Charts: Sales by Product Line, City-wise Revenue, Customer Type Analysis, Payment Method Distribution, and Sales Trends
- Filters: City, Product Line, Customer Type, Gender, and Payment Method for interactive drill-down

## Data Source

| File | Description |
|--------|------------|
| SuperMarket Analysis.csv | Sales transactions, product details, customer information, payment methods, ratings, and revenue data from supermarket operations |

## Key Insights

- Food & Beverages and Electronic Accessories generate significant sales revenue.
- Member customers contribute a large share of total purchases.
- E-wallet, Cash, and Credit Card are the primary payment methods used by customers.
- Certain cities consistently outperform others in sales generation.
- Higher-rated product lines tend to achieve stronger sales performance.
